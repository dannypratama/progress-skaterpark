# Design Direction: Progress Skatepark

> Brand direction and design system definition for Progress Skatepark website.

## 1. Identity & Mood

- **Product:** Skatepark facility & private course landing page in Tegal, Central Java.
- **Audience:** Local skateboard community, youth, beginners, and parents booking courses.
- **Visual Mood:** Raw street skateboarding energy meets clean, accessible functional layout.
- **Dial:** ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2

## 2. Color System

- **Primary:** `#dc3545` (Vibrant Red - energy, passion, skate culture focal points)
- **Secondary:** `#bcd3fc` (Ice Blue accent - balance and contrast)
- **Neutral Dark:** `#2c3031` (Deep Charcoal - typography and structure)
- **Neutral Light:** `#ffffff` (Clean white backgrounds)
- **Surface Accent:** `#f8f9fa` (Muted section separation)

## 3. Typography

- **Headings (Display/Accents):** `Rock Salt` (graffiti hand-drawn street spirit for hero/branding accents)
- **Body & Controls:** `Montserrat` (clean geometric sans-serif for high readability, 400/500/600/700)
- **Section Badges:** Arial / Helvetica sans-serif bold for background outline strokes

## 4. Interaction & Motion Rules

- **Hover/Focus:** High contrast state changes without disorienting extreme transforms.
- **3D Flip:** Supported with `:hover` and `:focus-within` for full keyboard accessibility.
- **Keyboard Support:** Visible focus indicator on all interactive controls.
